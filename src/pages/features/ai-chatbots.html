The question they didn’t want to phone about.

“Do you cover my area?” “How long does it take?” “Do I need to be home?” Most people will not call to ask. They will assume the answer is no and leave. A chatbot that actually knows your business answers in the moment and keeps the conversation going.

What's included

It answers from what you told it. Nothing else.

01

Grounded in your information

Services, coverage, process, timescales, policies, what you do not do. It answers from that, not from the open internet, so it cannot invent a service you do not offer.

02

Quick replies that go somewhere

Every answer offers the sensible next question or the next step. A visitor should never be left with a dead end and a back button.

03

A handover rule

You define what it must not answer. Those conversations are collected and passed to a person rather than guessed at.

04

Enquiry capture

When a conversation is ready to become a lead it takes the details in a structured form instead of leaving someone to find your contact page.

05

Written in your voice

The tone is set from how you already write to customers, so it does not read like a different company bolted onto your site.

What it deliberately doesn't do

  • It does not browse the web. If the answer is not in what you gave it, it says so and offers a person.
  • It does not quote prices you have not given it.
  • It does not pretend to be a human colleague.
  • It is not a support desk. Handling accounts, orders or existing cases needs real integrations, which we scope separately.

Questions

The things people ask before saying yes.

Will it make things up?

It answers from the information you supply and is built to say it does not know rather than fill the gap. That is a deliberate trade: fewer confident answers, no invented ones.

What happens to conversations it cannot finish?

They are captured with the visitor’s details and passed to you. An unanswered question you can see is far more useful than a visitor who quietly left.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, and it is designed for it — most of these conversations happen on a phone, so the widget is built for a thumb rather than shrunk from desktop.

How much work is it for me?

The writing is the work. Most of it already exists in your inbox as answers you have typed a hundred times.
