04 Discoverability

Each layer only works if the one under it does.

  1. 01

    Fix the foundation

    Speed, mobile rendering, clean markup, metadata, sitemaps, and making sure nothing is quietly blocking search engines from reading the site at all.

  2. 02

    Give it a shape

    One page per thing you actually sell, organised so both people and crawlers can tell what the site is about — with internal links that lead somewhere useful.

  3. 03

    Answer the real questions

    Content written for what people type when they need what you do — including the local phrasing, if you serve an area rather than the whole country.

  4. 04

    Make it easier to find

    Better foundations, clearer structure and content that matches intent give a site a fairer chance of being surfaced. No one can promise a position — anyone who does is guessing.

What's included

The work, in the order it has to happen.

01

Technical foundation

Crawlability, indexing, speed, mobile rendering, clean semantic markup, metadata, structured data and sitemaps. The unglamorous layer that everything else depends on.

02

Site structure

One page per thing you actually sell, organised so both people and crawlers can tell what the site is about, with internal links that lead somewhere useful rather than everywhere.

03

Content that matches intent

Pages written for what people type when they need what you do, including the phrasing they actually use, rather than the phrasing your industry prefers.

04

Local fundamentals

Consistent business information, service-area pages that are genuinely distinct, and the structured data that helps a search engine understand where you operate.

05

Measurement you can read

What changed, what moved, and what we would do next — in plain language, without a dashboard screenshot standing in for an explanation.

What it deliberately doesn't do

  • We do not guarantee rankings or traffic. Anyone who does is guessing with your money.
  • We do not buy links or use private blog networks.
  • We do not publish thin pages built for a search engine rather than a reader.
  • We do not promise a timescale for results. We will tell you what we are fixing and why it should help.

Questions

The things people ask before saying yes.

How long until it works?

Honestly: it varies enough that a number would be a guess. Technical fixes can register quickly; content and authority take longer. We would rather tell you what changed than invent a date.

Can you guarantee page one?

No, and neither can anyone else. Search engines rank on signals no agency controls. What we can do is make sure nothing about your site is holding it back.

Do I need new content every month?

Not automatically. A content retainer that produces pages nobody searches for is a subscription, not a strategy. We would rather fix structure first and write only where there is real intent.

What if my site is on a platform you did not build?

That is normal. We audit what is there and either fix it in place or tell you honestly when the platform itself is the constraint.
